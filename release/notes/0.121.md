## 0.121

A right-click picture keeps the clicked code in view, with the menu beside it. A screenshot's `cut:` lines and `"fit": true` cover a host Settings page in Split Mode. With highlights, `fit` only makes room for what the picture outlines, and it grows a window to 1.5 times its width at most.

### Right-click pictures

- A `click` with `symbol` lands just past the end of the word, so the context menu opens beside the code, not over it.
- A click point on the outline of another highlight joins that step: it shows a bare pointer with no badge or label, and the numbering skips it.
- Badges and labels keep off the lines of code in view around a code highlight.
- `"crop":"popups"` keeps every highlight and reaches left to the line numbers of the code.

### Cut content and fit

- With highlights, a cut counts only where they are: the rows they outline and the field, header or label they are on. Other long lines may stay cut, and `fit` leaves them alone.
- `fit` grows a window to 1.5 times its width at most, over all its rounds together. A `window` step's width from a `cut:` line is capped the same way.
- In Split Mode, a host Settings page's cuts give `cut:` lines. A window fix runs as a JetBrains Client window step, and a splitter fix runs on the backend. `{"action":"get","layout":true}` reports the layout problems of the showing windows, and `"within"` limits them to screen areas.
- A tree table's cut, such as the Inspections tree's, is measured from the rows in view.
- A cut in a pane of an IDE splitter with no room asks the window for enough width to cover the pane's share.
- Highlights are found again after a `fit` that resized something, so their outlines match the new layout.

### Fixes

- Two `fit` rounds in one screenshot left a window's saved size at the first round's result. The restore now puts back the size from before the fit.
