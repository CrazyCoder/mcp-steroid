## 0.124

`steroid_ui` arrows find empty space on pages with trees, such as Settings, and the report names the side the picture draws. In a `marks` screenshot, a tree row's ref keeps off the row's text.

### Arrows

![The Appearance page with three automatic arrows: from labels on empty space to the Appearance row of the Settings tree, the Theme combo box and the custom font checkbox](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CrazyCoder/mcp-steroid/v0.124/release/notes/img/0.124-arrows-trees.png)

- `auto` tries arrows up to 400 px long, so a label can reach the empty space past a tree's rows. It tried 1.5 and 2 times the arrow's length at most, and put labels over tree rows and over each other.
- The text of the tree rows in view counts as text: a label keeps off it, and a shaft does not cross it where another spot allows.
- A label keeps a 3 px margin from painted content, and covers no glyph. A long label could hide a whole letter.
- A label keeps off earlier arrows' shafts, and a shaft that crosses an earlier label counts as a crossing.
- When no spot is clear, a label on empty space wins over a shorter arrow whose label covers text. A shaft over empty space wins over one across painted text, such as a combo box's value.
- The report names the side and length the picture draws. On a scaled screen, the report and the picture measured the label differently, and could name different sides.

### Marks

![The Keymap page as a marks screenshot: each control with its ref, and each row of the Settings tree and the Actions tree with its row ref at its right end](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CrazyCoder/mcp-steroid/v0.124/release/notes/img/0.124-marks.png)

- A tree row whose text reaches the row's right end, such as "Build, Execution, Deployment" in the Settings tree, has its ref just past the text, not over it.
