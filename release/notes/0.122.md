## 0.122

`steroid_ui` highlights take arrows: the badge and label move to the arrow's tail, on empty space in the picture, and the arrow points back at the target. A highlight can go without its outline or its number, and a picture takes a color and a line width. `steroid_refactor` reads files changed on disk before it answers, and lists mentions in documents apart from code.

### Arrows

![Three numbered steps on the Appearance page: arrows point at the Theme combo box and the custom font checkbox, their labels on empty space](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CrazyCoder/mcp-steroid/v0.122/release/notes/img/0.122-numbered-arrows.png)

The picture above is one screenshot step: `"highlight":[{"text":"Theme:","arrow":true,"label":"pick a theme"},{"text":"Use custom font:","arrow":true,"label":"turn this on"},{"text":"Size:","label":"then set the size"}]`.

- `"arrow": true` places the arrow automatically. It tries right, left, below, above and the diagonals, at the arrow's length and then at 1.5 and 2 times it, and takes the first spot where the callout lies on empty space and the shaft crosses no text and no other outline.
- Empty space is read from the window as painted: a callout keeps off text, fields, combo boxes, icons and code, whatever component paints them.
- `"arrow": {"from": "left", "length": 80, "head": "open"}` forces the side (`left`, `right`, `above`, `below` or a diagonal such as `below-left`), the length (20 to 400 px, 60 by default) and the head (`filled`, `open` or `none`). A forced side flips at the picture's edge, and gets shorter when neither side fits.
- On a diagonal, the arrow leaves from the label's corner nearest the target.
- The report says where each arrow went, such as `arrow from below-right 60px`, and `flipped from left` or `shortened from 400px` when placement changed a forced side.

![The editor's context menu after a right click on orderTotal: the word as step 1 with the pointer on it, and an orange arrow from step 2's label to the Refactor item](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CrazyCoder/mcp-steroid/v0.122/release/notes/img/0.122-context-menu-arrow.png)

### Outlines, numbers and styles

- `"outline": false` draws the arrow without the outline, for a word in code or a small icon that a box would hide.
- `"number": false` leaves one step without a badge; the other steps count on without it.
- `"style": {"color": "orange", "width": 3}` on a highlight, or on the screenshot step for every highlight: `red` (the default), `orange`, `yellow`, `green`, `blue`, `purple`, `black` or `#RRGGBB`, and a width of 1 to 8 px. Text on a light color, such as yellow or orange, turns black.
- The breadcrumb takes an object form, `{"breadcrumb": true}`, so it can carry an arrow.

### steroid_refactor

- Each call saves the IDE's open documents and rereads files changed on disk first. After an edit made outside the IDE, `usages` used to list old line numbers and miss new usages, and an `apply` could change stale content.
- `usages` lists the lines of comments and documents that name the target, such as a Markdown code span, under "named in comments and documents", apart from the code.
- A dry run of `optimize_imports` or `reformat` lists the lines it would add and remove. For Kotlin it previews on a copy that resolves as the file does: it used to report an explicit import for every `kotlin.*` name.
- `inspect` and `fix` build no Inspection Results view. The platform added one to the Problems tool window and activated it, and the IDE logged "Tools are not initialized" once the run closed it.

### Fixes

- Label and badge text is centred on its glyphs. With the IDE's font it sat low, touching the bottom of the label.
- A soft-wrapped line of code keeps badges and labels off its whole width, on each of its visual lines.
- A crop to the Settings page, a tool window or a control grows to where each mark in it is drawn among all the marks.
